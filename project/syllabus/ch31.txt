# ---- grammar
X:crasis | crasis: a final vowel merges with the next word's initial vowel; the coronis (᾽) marks it: κἀγώ = καὶ ἐγώ, κἀκεῖνος = καὶ ἐκεῖνος, ταὐτά = τὰ αὐτά, τἀληθῆ = τὰ ἀληθῆ, ὦνδρες = ὦ ἄνδρες, ἐγᾦδα = ἐγὼ οἶδα, τοὔνομα = τὸ ὄνομα
X:ou-me | οὐ states a fact (objective), μή expresses will, wish, condition or purpose (subjective): a participle with μή is conditional (οἱ μὴ φιλοῦντες τὴν πόλιν “those who do not love the city” = “if anyone does not …”)
X:neg-double | two negatives: a compound negative after another negative strengthens it (οὐκ εἶπεν οὐδεὶς οὐδέν “no one said anything”); a simple οὐ after a compound one cancels it (οὐδεὶς οὐχ ὁρᾷ “everyone sees”)
X:me-fut-inf | verbs of hoping, promising and swearing + μή + future infinitive: ἐλπίζω αὐτὸν μὴ ἀφίξεσθαι “I hope he will not come”
τριακοντούτης | lasting thirty years (fem. τριακοντοῦτις: of a truce) |

X:hos-ptc | ὡς + participle: the reason or claim of the subject, “on the ground that, as if” (ὡς τοὺς νέους διαφθείροντα “on the ground that he was corrupting the young”); Smyth §2086
