X:nom-inf | indirect statement with the infinitive: when the subject is the same as that of the main verb it is not expressed, and what refers to it is nominative (ἔφη ἕτοιμος εἶναι “he said he was ready”)
X:attraction | attraction: a relative that should be accusative takes the case of a genitive or dative antecedent (ἄξιος τῆς τιμῆς ἧς ἔχει)
X:double-acc | verbs of teaching, asking, hiding, persuading take two accusatives: a person and a thing
X:prefix-assim | prefixes in compounds: ἐν- and συν- change before certain consonants (ἐμ-μένω, ἐγ-καλέω, συμ-, συγ-, συλ-) and come back before the augment (ἐν-έμεινα, ἐν-εκάλεσα)
X:demos-deme | ὁ δῆμος also means “deme”, one of the villages and districts of Attica
X:sense-demos | δῆμος also means “deme”, one of the districts of Attica | lemma=δῆμος hint=deme (district of Attica)
