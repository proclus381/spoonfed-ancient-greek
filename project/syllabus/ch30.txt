# ---- grammar: conditions
X:cond-gen-pres | general condition, present: ἐάν + subjunctive, present indicative: “if (ever) …, (always) …”
X:cond-flv | future “should … would” condition: εἰ + optative, ἄν + optative: “if he should come, I would …”
X:cond-gen-past | general condition, past: εἰ + optative, imperfect: “if (ever) …, (always) used to …”
X:cond-unreal-pres | unreal condition, present: εἰ + imperfect, ἄν + imperfect: “if he were (now) …, he would …”
X:cond-unreal-past | unreal condition, past: εἰ + aorist, ἄν + aorist: “if he had …, he would have …”
X:cond-indirect | conditions in indirect speech after a past verb: ἐάν + subj. → εἰ + opt.; the main verb becomes infinitive or optative (ἄν is kept)
# ---- grammar: verbal adjectives, the dual
X:verbal-teos | verbal adjective in -τέος, -τέα, -τέον: “must be done” (agent in the dative); impersonal -τέον ἐστί: ποιητέον ἐστίν ἡμῖν “we must act”
X:verbal-tos | verbal adjective in -τός: “done” or “that can be done” (γραπτός “written”, ἄγραφος “unwritten”, θνητός “mortal”)
ἅτε | inasmuch as, since (+ participle) |
# Smyth inventory (added 2026-11-23): Smyth concepts Athenaze does not treat

X:ind-pot | the past potential: ἄν + aorist or imperfect indicative outside a condition, “would have, could have” (ἔγνω ἄν τις “one could have seen”); Smyth §1784
X:an-iter | the iterative ἄν: ἄν + imperfect or aorist indicative for a repeated past action, “used to, would” (ἔλεγεν ἄν “he used to say”); Smyth §1790
X:wish-unreal | an unattainable wish: εἴθε / εἰ γάρ + imperfect (now) or aorist (past) indicative, or ὤφελον + infinitive (εἴθε ἔζη “if only he were alive!”); Smyth §1780–1781
X:ei-kai | concessive εἰ καί “even though” and καὶ εἰ (κἄν) “even if”; Smyth §2369–2372
X:cond-mixed | mixed conditions: the protasis and the apodosis of different types (εἰ τότε ἐπείσθης, νῦν ἂν εὐδαίμων ἦσθα “if you had obeyed then, you would be happy now”); Smyth §2355–2358
