X:gen-abs | the genitive absolute: a noun and a participle in the genitive, not tied to the rest of the sentence: “when / since / although …” (ἑσπέρας γιγνομένης “as evening was falling”)
X:art-adv | article + adverb: οἱ νῦν “the people of today”, οἱ τότε “the people of that time”, οἱ ἐνθάδε “the people here”
X:art-gen | article + genitive: Δικαιόπολις ὁ Ἀρχελάου “Dikaiopolis son of Archelaos”; τὰ τῆς πόλεως “the affairs of the city”
X:art-prep | article + prepositional phrase: οἱ ἐν τῷ ἄστει “the people in the city”; τὰ ἐν τῇ οἰκίᾳ “the things in the house”
X:art-repeat | attributive position with the article repeated: ὁ ἀγρὸς ὁ τοῦ γείτονος “the neighbour's field”
X:pred-pos | predicative position: an adjective outside article + noun is a predicate: καλὸν ἔχει τὸ πρόσωπον “the face she has is beautiful” (= her face is beautiful)
X:kata-gen | κατά + genitive: “down from; down upon; against” (κατὰ τοῦ ὄρους “down from the mountain”)
X:gen-price | the genitive of price: πόσου; “for how much?”, δραχμῆς “for a drachma”
X:hos-while | ὡς = ἐν ᾧ: “while, as” (ὡς ἐβάδιζον … “as they were walking …”)
