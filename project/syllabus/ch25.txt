X:ei-gar | εἰ γάρ + optative: a wish, “if only, would that” (= εἴθε)
X:logos-ergon | λόγῳ μὲν … ἔργῳ δὲ …: “in word … but in fact …”
X:opt-wish | the optative of wish (often with εἴθε or εἰ γάρ; negative μή): “may …!, if only …!”
X:opt-past | after a past tense the subjunctive of a subordinate clause may become optative (ἵνα, μή after verbs of fearing, ὅτε, ὅστις …), and ἄν drops out (ἐάν → εἰ, ὅταν → ὅτε)
X:opt-indirect | indirect speech after a past tense: the verb may go into the optative of the same tense (ἔλεγεν ὅτι ὁ πατὴρ νοσοίη “he said that his father was ill”)
